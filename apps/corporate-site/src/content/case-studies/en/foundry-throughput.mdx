## Context

Brackenhill Metals (fictional) runs a 140,000 tonne-per-year foundry with three casting lines. OEE had drifted to 61%, scrap was above 8%, and the team was preparing a capital request for a fourth furnace.

The plant manager suspected the furnace was the constraint. The production data suggested something else: the line lost more time between casts than during them.

## Approach

A two-week diagnostic combined time studies, changeover video, and a scrap Pareto by alloy and shift.

- 38% of changeover delay was waiting for tooling and pre-heat checks that could run in parallel.
- Two alloy grades accounted for 61% of scrap, both linked to a missing pre-cast temperature check.
- The schedule grouped grades in a way that forced unnecessary cleanouts.

We moved pre-heat to a parallel station, built an external setup checklist, and changed the scheduling rule to group compatible grades. No furnace was ordered.

## Results

- OEE moved from 61% to 78% over four months.
- Scrap fell from 8.4% to 3.1%.
- Changeover dropped from 42 minutes to 18 minutes.

The capital request was withdrawn. The plant instead spent roughly $180,000 (fictional) on fixtures, a pre-heat station, and operator training.

## What changed

The durable change was a ten-minute daily line review with one metric: minutes lost between casts. Six months later, the plant still runs the same checklist and schedule rule.
